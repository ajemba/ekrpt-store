Privacy & Cookie Policy

EKRPT Networking Labs Ltd. ("EKRPT", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, how we use cookies and analytics, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email, shipping address, and order details when you make a purchase or contact us — and limited technical information collected automatically, such as your device type, browser, and how you use the site.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders and provide customer support.
  • To respond to enquiries you send through the contact page or email.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it.
  • To keep the site and your transactions secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use two kinds:

  • Essential cookies — needed for the site to work, such as keeping you signed in and remembering your cart. These are always active.
  • Analytics cookies — used with Google Analytics to understand which pages are visited and how the site performs. These run only if you accept them.

When you first visit, a banner lets you Accept all cookies or continue with Essentials only. Until you accept, analytics cookies stay switched off. You can change your choice at any time using the button below.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment providers (such as PayPal and Cryptomus). Your full payment details are handled by them directly and are never stored on our servers.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the store — payment processors, delivery partners, and analytics — and only as far as needed to provide the service, or where required by law.

Your choices

  • Accept or decline analytics cookies through the consent banner or the cookie settings link above.
  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you, or request that we correct or delete it.
  • Unsubscribe from any marketing email using the link in the message.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact us at office@ekrpt.com or through our contact page.
